This privacy policy explains which personal data the RunFromMe app processes, why, for how long, and which rights you have. RunFromMe is being developed as a school project at HTL Spengergasse (Vienna).

Overview

  • Account: e-mail address (not for guests), display name, username, optional bio, avatar, the confirmation that you are old enough.
  • Playing: matches you took part in (mode, role, result, distance, catches, duration), XP, rewards, achievements, items.
  • Location: only during the play-area setup and a running match; deleted at the end of the match.
  • Friends: friend requests, friendships, blocks, your friend code, lobby invites.
  • Safety: reports you make or that concern you, moderation decisions.
  • Support: messages you send us, and – only if you choose – technical details and screenshots.
  • Technical: devices you are signed in on (platform, model, app version), crash reports, usage analytics (only with your yes).

Account and profile

To play online you create an account with e-mail and password, sign in with a provider such as Google or Apple (if offered), or play as a guest without an e-mail address. We store your e-mail address (for sign-in, password reset and important account notices), your display name, username, optional bio and the look you chose.

Instead of your date of birth we only store that you confirmed you are at least the minimum age (16) and when. Your date of birth never leaves your phone.

Other players see your display name, username, bio, look and the statistics you share – what exactly is up to your privacy settings (Profile → Settings → Privacy).

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – the account is needed to use the online features.

Location

The location is the heart of the game and is handled with particular care:

  • It is used only while you choose a play area as host and during a running match – never in the background and never outside a match.
  • During a match your phone sends your position to the game server about every 3 seconds. The server uses it to decide catches, scans and the play-area rules.
  • Nobody sees your live position. Hunters only see a few seconds old snapshot of Runners every 45 seconds; Runners only see a distance band.
  • When the match ends, all positions of the match are deleted. Results only contain distance, time and catches – never coordinates or routes.
  • If your phone reports a position as coming from a location-faking app, we only note that it happened (no position), for fair play.

App: Privacy → Location

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – without location the game cannot be played. Fair-play checks: legitimate interest in fair matches (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Matches, progress and friends

We store the matches you played, your XP and season progress, rewards, achievements and items, and the people in your Crew (friendships, requests, blocks, invites). Whether you are online or in a lobby or match is not stored: it is sent live to your friends only, and only as far as your privacy settings allow.

Your in-app inbox (friend requests, invites, rewards) is deleted after 30 days at the latest.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Safety and moderation

If you report a player, we store the reason, your optional explanation and, if given, the match. The reported player never learns who reported them. Our team decides on warnings, suspensions or bans – nothing is decided automatically. Names and bios are checked against a list of words that are not allowed.

Every decision of the team is recorded in an audit log.

Community Guidelines

Legal basis: legitimate interest in a safe community and in enforcing the Terms of Use (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Support

When you send a bug report, feedback or a contact request, we store your message and – only if you choose – a reply e-mail address, technical details (app version, Android version, device model, language, last screen) and up to three screenshots you picked. Resolved requests are deleted after 180 days.

Legal basis: performance of the contract and, for the optional details, your consent (Art. 6(1)(b) and (a) GDPR).

Crash reports and usage analytics

  • Crash reports: when the app crashes we receive the error, where in the app it happened, the last screens, the app version, the operating system and the device model – never your location or anything you typed. They are kept for 90 days. You can switch them off at any time in the privacy settings. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); switching them off is your objection.
  • Usage analytics: only if you say yes, we count which features are used (for example "match finished" with the mode and the number of players). Switching it off deletes what was collected; otherwise it is kept for up to 400 days. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

Both go to our own server only. RunFromMe contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and shows no advertising.

Devices and security

For each signed-in device we store the platform, device model and app version, so you can see your sessions and we can tell you about a new sign-in. If you turn on two-step verification, its key is stored by our authentication service. Rate limits and security checks process your account id and the time of requests.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in protecting your account and our service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Maps

The map tiles come from the servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom). To load them, your phone sends its IP address and the map area being shown to these servers; the OpenStreetMap Foundation is responsible for this processing itself. The United Kingdom has an adequacy decision of the European Commission.

OpenStreetMap privacy policy

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – the game needs a map.

Recipients and processors

  • Supabase Inc. hosts our database, sign-in, e-mails and file storage as our processor, on servers in Frankfurt (EU). Supabase is a US company; any access from outside the EU is covered by the EU standard contractual clauses in its data processing agreement.
  • GitHub Inc. (USA) stores our encrypted backups for 30 days as our processor, under the EU standard contractual clauses; without a key only we hold they cannot be read.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider learns that you signed in to RunFromMe.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store deliver the app under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep data

  • Positions: until the end of the match (at most 2 minutes of samples while it runs).
  • Account, profile, matches, progress, friends: until you delete your account.
  • Inbox: 30 days. Support requests: 180 days after they are resolved. Crash reports: 90 days. Usage analytics: up to 400 days or until you switch them off.
  • Reports and moderation decisions: as long as needed for safety; when you delete your account they stay linked only to an anonymous "Deleted player".
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in our encrypted backups for up to 30 days and in our hosting provider's backups for up to 7 days, then it is gone.

Deleting your account

In the app: Privacy → Delete account & data. The deletion happens 7 days later; until then you can cancel it. After that your login and your data are deleted. If you played matches with others, your profile stays only as an anonymous "Deleted player", so the other players' history stays complete.

Without the app you can request the deletion on our website.

Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future.

You can download all your data in the app (Privacy → Download my data). For everything else, contact us.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna.
