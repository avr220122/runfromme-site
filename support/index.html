Support & contact

Draft: the contact address is filled in before publication.

Fastest: in the app

  • Report a bug: Help → Report a bug (with a screenshot and technical details if you like).
  • Question or feedback: Help → Contact. The app shows you when we work on your request.
  • Someone behaves unfairly or inappropriately: on their profile → Report, or Help → Report a player.
