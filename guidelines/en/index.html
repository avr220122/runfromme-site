RunFromMe is played in the real world, with people you may not know well. These guidelines keep matches fun and everyone safe. They add to the Terms of Use; if something here and the Terms disagree, the Terms apply.

Terms of Use

Stay safe

  • Watch the traffic. Never run onto roads, tracks or into places you would not enter without the game.
  • Stay off private and restricted property, building sites and closed areas.
  • Respect people who are not playing: don't chase, scare or film them.
  • Stop a match whenever something feels wrong. Leaving is always fine.

App: Help → Safety

Play fair

  • Only your real location counts: no GPS faking, mock locations, emulators or modified apps.
  • One account per person. No playing for someone else, no account sharing or selling.
  • No bots, scripts or automation.
  • Don't team up with the other side to fix a match.

Respect others

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, hate or discrimination – for any reason.
  • No stalking: don't use RunFromMe to follow, track or find someone outside a match.
  • No sexual content and nothing that endangers children. RunFromMe is 16+.

Names and profiles

Display names, @usernames and bios are seen by others. They must not be offensive, sexual, hateful, impersonate other people or brands, or contain links, phone numbers or other contact details.

Reporting and blocking

  • Blocking a player hides you from each other in Crew and keeps them out of your lobbies. They are not told.
  • Report a player from their profile. Pick the reason that fits and add what happened. The reported player never learns who reported them.
  • Report honestly. Reports made to harass someone are themselves against these guidelines.

App: Report a player

What happens

The RunFromMe team looks at reports – several reports never lead to a sanction on their own; a person decides. Depending on how serious it is:

  1. Warning – shown in the app once.
  2. Temporary suspension – you can still open the app, see your data, export or delete your account, but you cannot play or interact until it ends.
  3. Permanent ban – for serious or repeated violations.

Offensive names or bios can be reset. Every decision is recorded.

Questions and appeals

If you think a decision was wrong, tell us through Help & Feedback. Please include your @username.
