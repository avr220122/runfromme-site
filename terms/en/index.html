RunFromMe is a location-based chase game you play with other people in real life. RunFromMe is being developed as a school project at HTL Spengergasse. These terms describe the rules for using the app.

Scope

These Terms of Use apply to the RunFromMe app and all of its features: matches, profiles, Crew, events, rewards and Help & Feedback.

How RunFromMe handles your data, in particular your location, is described in the privacy policy.

Privacy policy

Account & participation

  • To play online matches you need a profile with a display name and a username.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else and do not use names meant to mislead others.
  • One account per person is intended.
  • You are responsible for what happens through your profile. Do not share your access with others.

Playing RunFromMe

A match takes place in a play area that the host sets before the start. Runners try to escape the Hunters; Hunters try to catch Runners.

  • Taking part in a match is voluntary.
  • You can leave a match at any time without giving a reason.
  • Results and statistics are calculated from the data the server confirms during the match.

Safety in real life

RunFromMe is played in public spaces. The app cannot judge your surroundings. You decide yourself where and whether it is safe to run.

While playing, always:

  • Never run into the road and follow the traffic rules.
  • Do not enter private property, construction sites or railway areas, not even to hide.
  • Do not take risky shortcuts, do not climb and avoid unsafe ground.
  • Never play while driving a car or riding a bike.
  • Watch your path and other people, not just your phone.

RunFromMe is not an emergency service. If you are in acute danger, call the emergency number 112 immediately.

App: Help → Safety

Location & game mechanics

Matches only work with your location. Without location access you cannot play a match.

  • Your location is only sent to the server during a running match.
  • Others never see your position live, only as the game rules allow.
  • When a match ends, the positions of that match are deleted.

The privacy policy explains this in more detail.

App: Privacy → Location

Conduct & community

Treat others the way you want to be treated. In particular, the following is not allowed:

  • harassing, insulting or threatening other players
  • spam and advertising
  • offensive or inappropriate names, usernames and profile texts
  • luring or following someone into dangerous or forbidden areas
  • using matches to meet someone outside the game who does not want that
  • false or deliberately abusive reports

If you feel uncomfortable, you can block or report a person. The reported person does not find out who reported them.

App: Report a player

Fair play & cheating

A match is only fair if everyone really runs. In particular, the following is not allowed:

  • faking your location, for example with fake GPS apps
  • bots, scripts or other automation
  • modifying the app or manipulating data sent to the server
  • deliberately exploiting bugs instead of reporting them
  • using several accounts to gain an advantage
  • arranging matches or manipulating results
  • getting around a ban with a new account

The server checks reported positions for plausibility and can discard implausible positions.

App: Help → Report a bug

User content

Your content includes your display name, your username, your profile text and everything you send through Help & Feedback or reports.

  • Your content must not infringe the rights of others and must not break the rules under "Conduct & community".
  • Certain usernames are reserved and cannot be chosen.
  • Content that breaks these terms can be removed or reset.

Rewards & events

In RunFromMe you can unlock skins, styles, emotes, poses and titles, for example through the season, achievements, events or collab goals.

  • Rewards are digital items with no monetary value.
  • Nothing can be bought in RunFromMe. Rewards cannot be sold, traded or transferred.
  • A reward counts as claimed once the server has confirmed the claim.
  • Seasons and events are limited in time. Their content and rewards can change.

Availability

RunFromMe is in development. Features can change, be temporarily missing or still work with sample data. Online matches need an internet connection.

Measures for violations

If these Terms of Use are broken, the RunFromMe team can remove content, reset names, restrict participation in matches or suspend an account temporarily or permanently.

Changes

These Terms of Use have a version number and a date, both shown at the top of this page. If a new version needs your agreement, the app asks you before you continue playing. Agreement is never assumed silently.

Contact

You can ask questions about these Terms of Use through Help & Feedback.
